✈️ Transit Guide

How to Arrive from KLIA Airport (T1 / T2)

Follow this simple step-by-step train connection to reach Rain Forest Hotel smoothly in ~40 minutes.

01
KLIA T1 / T2 Airport ⚡ KLIA Ekspres

Board the non-stop KLIA Ekspres train from Terminal 1 or Terminal 2 straight to KL Sentral (~28 mins).

↓
02
KL Sentral Station 🚈 LRT Kelana Jaya Line (1 Stop)

Transfer to the LRT Kelana Jaya Line (heading towards Gombak) and ride just 1 stop to Pasar Seni.

↓
03
Pasar Seni Station (MRT / LRT) 🚶 Short Walk

Exit via Pasar Seni Station (Exit A / Petaling St exit). Walk ~2 minutes (200m) directly down Petaling Street.
