Our Philosophy

Budget comfort without the compromise.

We built Rain Forest Hotel because we were tired of the standard budget experience. You shouldn't have to choose between a central location and a good night's sleep, or between affordable rates and a beautifully kept space.

There are no generic templates and no hidden fees here. Just thoughtfully curated rooms tucked safely away in the center of KL's most vibrant district—a 2-minute walk from the chaos of Petaling Street and right around the corner from Pasar Seni station.

Need to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

What time is check-in and check-out?

Check-in is from 14:00, and check-out is until 12:00. Express check-in/out is available.

Is parking available on-site?

No, there is no parking available on-site. We recommend guests arriving by car or Grab plan accordingly.

Do you serve breakfast?

While we don't have a full restaurant, you can find a delicious breakfast at our courtyard, featuring banana bread, eggs, and toast, or step right outside to explore Chinatown's incredible food scene.

How do I get there from the airport?

The easiest way from KLIA is to take the KLIA Ekspres train to KL Sentral, then transfer to the Kelana Jaya LRT line for one stop to Pasar Seni. We are a 4-minute walk from there.
